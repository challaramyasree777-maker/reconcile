## 2. What this Service is

Reconcile is an agentic bookkeeping tool. It:

- Fetches bank transactions from Plaid's API
- Uses a large language model (Groq) to propose vendor categorisations
- Routes transactions through an automated decision graph (LangGraph)
- Posts approved journal entries to QuickBooks
- Records a full audit trail of every agent decision and human correction

Reconcile is a **workflow automation tool**. It does not provide accounting, legal, tax, or financial advice. All AI-generated categorisation proposals are suggestions only; human review and approval are part of the designed workflow.
